Assault in Japan - Hostage Rescue
by Luisinho (luisinhocontactservices@gmail.com)

Counter-Terrorists: Breach the
Black Mesa Cargo Center in this small japanese
town and rescue the hostages. Use the truck's
camera monitors to watch the Terrorists'
position inside the facility.

Terrorists: You have taken four of the most
important Black Mesa scientists hostages, 
prevent Counter-Terrorists from rescuing 
them. Use the camera system to track their
movements
